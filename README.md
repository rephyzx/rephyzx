**# rephyzx — Physics for Everyone

**Making physics easier to understand, explore, and experience.**

rephyzx is a long-term educational initiative dedicated to making physics accessible to learners of all ages and backgrounds.

## Our Vision

To build a comprehensive physics learning ecosystem, from fundamental concepts to advanced topics, using clear explanations, visualizations, interactive simulations, and hands-on experiments.

## What We're Building

- 📚 Physics lessons for beginners and advanced learners
- 🔬 Interactive physics simulations
- 💡 Visual explanations of complex concepts
- 🧮 Mathematics foundations for physics
- 🤖 AI-assisted learning tools
- 🌍 Accessible educational resources for everyone

## Current Status

This project is in its early development stage. The initial focus is on learning, research, planning, and building a strong foundation for future educational resources.

## Long-Term Mission

To help people around the world develop a deeper understanding of physics through technology, creativity, and accessible education.

*Learning physics. Exploring the universe. Sharing knowledge.***
